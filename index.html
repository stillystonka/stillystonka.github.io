Our Mission

Creating pathways to education and empowerment

Building a Brighter Future Together

AESOP provides free, volunteer-taught, online courses to Afghan girls and young women deprived of education in Afghanistan and across the diaspora.

Afghans and foreigners working together create a community of service, find their power, pursue their education, learn their value, and work towards a brighter future for themselves, their families, and their country.

If you have English and you have the internet, there's nothing you can't learn.

- Zahra Yazdani, AESOP co-founder

The Crisis

Understanding the urgent need for our work

3.7 Million

Girls deprived of education after the Taliban banned girls from school after 6th grade in August 2021.

100,000+

Women removed from universities when private education centers were closed in December 2022.

Only Country

Afghanistan is the only country in the world where a 12-year-old girl cannot go to school by governmental decree.

The Reality: Having lived their whole lives under U.S. occupation with expectations of learning, contribution, and self-fulfillment, young women and girls of Afghanistan now face an increasingly bleak future.

Without intervention, this and subsequent generations of girls and women will be lost to a life of ignorance and illiteracy. Without an educated and critical-thinking womankind, poverty, radicalization and violence will continue to increase.

Student Success Stories

Lives transformed through education

Husna

Co-Founder, Assistant Director/Academic Program Director

Started as a student in June 2022 after being turned away from the University of Balkh at gunpoint. Spent nearly two years working from her bedroom, overlooking a Taliban outpost, to help girls learn. Now an Ada Comstock Scholar at Smith College majoring in Government and Economics.

Malika & Hasina

Sisters, Medical Student & Leadership Scholar

Two sisters who began with AESOP under very difficult conditions. Malika (23) has gone on to continue medical school at the University of Glasgow sponsored by the Linda Norgrove Foundation. Hasina (13) is now at the School of Leadership Afghanistan in Rwanda.

Mahtab

Director of Student Affairs

Began her AESOP journey in our first class before enrolling at the Asian University for Women in Bangladesh. Came to the US with her family on a Special Immigrant Visa and is still pursuing her education while serving as Director of Student Affairs at AESOP.

Fatima Amiri

BBC Top 100 Women of 2022

Joined as a mentee in spring 2023 after evacuating Afghanistan and receiving multiple surgical procedures after a terror attack on the Kaaj Education center. Already a voice and inspiration for Afghan girls worldwide, Fatima continues learning English with AESOP.

How We Support Students

💰 Financial Support

100% of funding goes to purchasing and distributing cellular data credit bundles so students and Afghan volunteers in extreme poverty can attend/teach classes.

We also provide free DuoLingo tests to our top students in the DET Prep course.

No money is sent directly to students in Afghanistan.

🤝 Social Support

AESOP's network has become a source of well-being, value, and belonging that relieves the oppression of isolation and social restrictions.

Students grow hope from within and nourish their commitment and determination. Afghan volunteers abroad rediscover their value in giving back.

🛡️ Safety & Well-Being

Our applications screen students to ensure engaging in our classes won't put them in danger from inside or outside their home.

Online studying is safer than secret in-person education and compares favorably to former in-person education where schools became targets of terror attacks.
