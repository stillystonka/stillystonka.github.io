English Language Courses

📖

Basic English

Beginner

What you'll learn: Start from the ABCs and build foundational English skills. Perfect for students with little to no English experience.

Topics include: Alphabet, basic vocabulary, simple grammar, introductory conversation skills

Who it's for: Complete beginners or students looking to strengthen their foundation

📝

Intermediate English

Intermediate

What you'll learn: Build on basic skills with more complex grammar, expanded vocabulary, and practical communication.

Topics include: Advanced grammar structures, reading comprehension, writing paragraphs, conversational fluency

Who it's for: Students who have completed Basic English or have some English experience

✍️

Advanced English/Literature

Advanced

What you'll learn: Master academic writing, literary analysis, and advanced discourse. Prepare for university-level English.

Topics include: Essay writing, critical analysis, literary texts, academic vocabulary, research skills

Who it's for: Students with strong English skills preparing for higher education

🎯

DuoLingo Test Prep

Test Preparation

What you'll learn: Prepare for the DuoLingo English Proficiency Test, essential for university applications and scholarships.

Topics include: Test format, strategies for each section, practice tests, time management

Bonus: Top students receive free DuoLingo tests!

Requirements & FAQ

What You Need

  • ✓ Internet access (we can help if needed)
  • ✓ Computer, tablet, or smartphone
  • ✓ Desire to learn and commitment to attend classes
  • ✓ Safe environment to study from home
  • ✓ Basic understanding that classes are in English (we teach from the beginning!)

Who Can Apply

  • ✓ Afghan girls and young women
  • ✓ Students in Afghanistan or across the diaspora
  • ✓ Any age (most students are 12-30)
  • ✓ All educational backgrounds welcome
  • ✓ Students who can safely participate without danger

Is AESOP really free?

Yes! 100% free. All courses are volunteer-taught and completely free to students. If you need help with internet access, we provide cellular data bundles.

How do I get internet access if I can't afford it?

We provide free cellular data credit bundles to students in financial need. Indicate your need in the application and we'll work with you.

What if I've never learned English before?

Perfect! Our Basic English course starts from the ABCs. Many of our most successful students started with no English knowledge.

How much time do I need to commit?

This varies by course, but typically expect 3-6 hours per week including class time and homework. You can take multiple courses if you have time.

Can I become a teacher later?

Yes! More than 25 of our advanced students have become volunteer teachers. It's a great way to practice skills and prepare for a teaching career.

Will AESOP help me get a scholarship abroad?

While we can't guarantee scholarships, we provide English skills and academic preparation that strengthen applications. 15 of our alumnae have received scholarships abroad!
