Husna's Journey to Smith College

From Bedroom in Balkh to Ada Comstock Scholar

At 25, Husna was turned away from the University of Balkh at gunpoint after the Taliban takeover. With donor support for internet access, she spent nearly two years teaching from her bedroom overlooking a Taliban outpost.

Today: Husna is an Ada Comstock Scholar at Smith College, majoring in Government and Economics. She continues her work with AESOP as Co-Founder and Academic Program Director.

Your $180 could support the next Husna.

Malika & Hasina: Sisters Reaching Their Dreams

From Difficult Conditions to Medical School & Leadership Academy

Malika (23) and Hasina (13) began with AESOP under very difficult conditions. Thanks to donor support providing them internet access and English education, both sisters have achieved remarkable success.

Today: Malika is continuing medical school at the University of Glasgow, sponsored by the Linda Norgrove Foundation. Hasina is at the School of Leadership Afghanistan in Rwanda.

Your gift helped two sisters - imagine what more we can do together.

Fatima Amiri: Rising from Tragedy

BBC Top 100 Women of 2022

After surviving a terror attack on the Kaaj Education Center in September 2022 that took her left eye and ear, Fatima evacuated Afghanistan. Already recognized as one of BBC's top 100 women, she refused to give up on education.

Today: With donor support, Fatima is learning English with AESOP and inspiring Afghan girls worldwide. She's a voice of hope and resilience.

Your support helps survivors thrive, not just survive.

From Students to Teachers

25+ Afghan Students Became Volunteer Teachers

More than 25 of our advanced Afghan students have become volunteer teachers, passing on knowledge to their Afghan sisters and preparing for careers in teaching - one of the few opportunities open to women in Afghanistan.

Impact Multiplied: When you support one student, you're investing in a future teacher who will educate dozens more. Your donation creates a ripple effect of learning.

One student today becomes ten teachers tomorrow.

Why AESOP is Different

Exceptional impact for every dollar donated

🎯 Targeted Impact

We fill a critical gap in the educational landscape. While large institutions provide college-level courses, fewer programs prepare girls for college readiness.

AESOP focuses on foundational education that prepares students for higher learning opportunities.

💪 Cost-Effective

With just $5 per student per year, we're one of the most cost-effective programs supporting Afghan girls' education.

From Summer 2022 to Summer 2024: $9,000 advanced 900 students - an incredible per-dollar impact!

🤝 Volunteer-Powered

All courses are taught by volunteers from the US, Central Asia, and the Middle East. No overhead for salaries means more money for students.

110 volunteers have given their time, expertise, and passion to make this possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

AESOP Afghanistan, Inc. is a registered nonprofit organization. Your donation may be tax-deductible. You will receive a receipt for your donation for tax purposes.

How is my money used?

100% of donations go directly to purchasing cellular data credit bundles for students and Afghan volunteers in extreme poverty, and to providing free DuoLingo tests for top students. No money is sent directly to individuals.

Can I sponsor a specific student?

While we don't offer individual sponsorships for safety and privacy reasons, your donation directly supports students in need. We can provide updates on the overall impact of your gift.

How do I know my donation is making a difference?

We provide regular updates to donors about program achievements, student success stories, and the number of students served. With our incredible $5 per student per year impact, you can be confident your donation changes lives.

Can I make a monthly recurring donation?

Yes! Monthly donations help us plan better and provide consistent support to our students. You can indicate your preference for monthly giving in the donation form.

Are there other ways to support besides money?

Absolutely! You can volunteer as a teacher or administrator. We're always looking for passionate volunteers to help create and teach courses.
