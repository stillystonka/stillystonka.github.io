Setting Up Your Merch Store

We recommend using Bonfire for your AESOP merch store.

Why Bonfire?

  • Perfect for non-profits and causes
  • No upfront costs or inventory required
  • Print-on-demand (only print what's sold)
  • Easy fundraising setup with transparent profit tracking
  • Professional quality products
  • Simple embeddable store widget

Quick Setup Steps:

  1. Go to bonfire.com and create an account
  2. Click "Start a Campaign" and choose "Sell & Fundraise"
  3. Upload your AESOP logo and design your products
  4. Set your profit margin (recommend $10-15 per item)
  5. Get your store link or embed code
  6. Replace this section with your Bonfire store embed

Embed Code:

Bonfire provides an embed code that looks like this:

<iframe src="https://www.bonfire.com/store/YOUR-STORE-NAME"></iframe>

Create Your Bonfire Store

Alternative Platforms:

  • Printful + Shopify: More control, requires Shopify subscription ($29/month)
  • Teespring (Spring): Similar to Bonfire, also free
  • CustomCat + WooCommerce: For WordPress sites
